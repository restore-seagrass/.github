# Duke RESTORE Seagrass Team

We develop and test practical, scalable approaches to seagrass restoration at the Duke University Marine Lab in Beaufort, North Carolina. Our research combines field and laboratory experiments, aquaculture, and monitoring to improve restoration outcomes and support coastal resilience.

Our work focuses on:

- **Seed-based restoration:** improving seed production, storage, and planting methods.
- **Species interactions:** testing whether clams and other facilitative interactions improve seagrass establishment and growth.
- **Restoration performance:** evaluating how planting methods and environmental conditions influence restoration success.

This organization brings together the team's project repositories, including code, analytical workflows, and documentation supporting our seagrass research.
